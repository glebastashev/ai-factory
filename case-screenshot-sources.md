## Original financial models (versions 1 and 3)

All values are hypothetical inputs. No client names, completed-project claims or guaranteed results have been added. Each card labels its effect as a model and links to volumes, costs, review steps, break-even conditions and illustrative setup budgets.

- B2B: 600 requests × (30% − 20%) paid conversion × 15,000 RUB contribution margin − 120,000 RUB monthly running cost = 780,000 RUB/month. A conversion increase is a pilot hypothesis, not an observed result.
- Documents: 18,000 documents × (45 − 12) RUB paid handling cost − 90,000 RUB monthly running cost = 504,000 RUB/month. A lower paid handling rate is a model assumption.
- Support: 18,000 paid conversations × 70 RUB − 9,000 manually handled conversations × 70 RUB − 60,000 RUB monthly running cost = 570,000 RUB/month. A 50% fully resolved automation rate is a pilot hypothesis.
